PROJECT: DubVerse

ROLE EXPECTATION:
Verdant is acting as a senior backend engineer providing implementation guidance only.
No ownership, no architectural authority, no training rights are granted.

MODULE UNDER REVIEW:
Audio diarization and decoding reliability

CURRENT PIPELINE (HIGH LEVEL):
1. Video uploaded to API
2. Audio extracted using FFmpeg
3. Audio passed to pyannote.audio for diarization
4. Speaker segments forwarded downstream

OBSERVED ISSUES:
- torchcodec warning when not installed
- Audio decoding fails in some environments
- Diarization crashes instead of degrading gracefully
- Failure stops the entire dubbing job

CONSTRAINTS:
- Windows and Linux support
- FFmpeg may or may not be present
- torchcodec cannot be required
- System must fail gracefully

GOALS:
- Robust audio decoding abstraction
- Fallback paths if decoding fails
- Clear error reporting
- Non-fatal failures where possible

REQUESTED OUTPUT:
1. Diagnosis of likely failure points
2. Recommended fallback strategy
3. Reference implementation or pseudocode
4. Risks and tradeoffs
